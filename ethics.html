IT Ethics

Ethics in information technology focuses on how technology should be used responsibly. IT professionals often work with sensitive data, so it is important to follow ethical guidelines.

Privacy

Protecting user privacy is essential. Personal information should only be collected when necessary and must be kept secure.

Security

Systems should be designed to prevent unauthorized access and protect against cyberattacks.

Responsibility

IT professionals should act honestly and be accountable for their work, including reporting issues and following policies.
